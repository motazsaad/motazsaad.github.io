التاريخ

A History of Beauty & Resilience

~ 5th c. CE

The Classical Roots

Arabic emerges in the Arabian Peninsula, carried on the tongues of poets who gathered at the famous suq of Ukaz for verse competitions.

610 CE

The Revelation

The Quran is revealed in Arabic. Its language becomes sacred and unites a vast region under a shared literary tongue.

8th–13th c.

The Golden Age

Arabic becomes the lingua franca of science and philosophy. Scholars in Baghdad, Cordoba, and Cairo write in it — from algebra to astronomy.

Modern Era

Modern Standard Arabic

A standardized form, descended from Classical Arabic, serves as the common written language of news, literature, and education across the Arab world.

النحو والصرف

Grammar from a Root

🌱

Root System

Most words grow from three-letter roots. From كتب (k-t-b, "to write") spring كتاب (book), مكتبة (library), and كاتب (writer).

🪞

Root & Pattern

Vowel patterns shape meaning: the pattern فاعِل signals the doer, while مفعول signals the receiver — a deeply logical, musical system.

🔤

Short Vowels

Short vowels are usually unwritten and inferred from context. The حركات (diacritics) are added in the Quran, poetry, and children's books for clarity.

✒️

Calligraphy

Because the script is cursive and flowing, it became an art form — from Kufic's angular forms to the sweeping curves of Naskh and Diwani.
